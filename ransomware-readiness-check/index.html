Ransomware Reality Check

Many businesses feel prepared, but recovery is often untested.

Many small businesses believe they are protected because they have backups, antivirus, or an IT provider. But ransomware readiness is not only about having tools in place. The real question is whether the business can recover files, appointments, invoices, emails, and systems quickly if access is suddenly blocked.

This free readiness check helps you understand:

  • Whether your backups are actually ready to support recovery
  • If important accounts are protected with strong access controls
  • Where email and phishing risks may affect your team
  • Whether your business has a clear first-hour response plan
  • What practical steps can improve your ransomware readiness
